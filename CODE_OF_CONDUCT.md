# Community conduct

Help other learners understand the work. Discuss ideas and code respectfully, explain corrections, and make room for people with different levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted contact, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not share anyone's credentials, private messages, or unpublished work.

Report concerns privately through the course or repository maintainer's existing contact channel. Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation, or exclude a participant to protect the learning community. Treat reports discreetly and avoid retaliation.
